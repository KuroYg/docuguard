# Terms of use

**Product:** DocuGuard preview with optional hosted accounts
**Last updated:** 19 November 2026

## What the preview does

DocuGuard compares local Git revisions, checks a focused Python shipping-multiplier rule against tracked Markdown, and shows evidence with a suggested correction. Hosted sign-in is available only when the operator has configured an identity provider. The analyzer does not apply corrections automatically.

## Use it responsibly

Only analyze repositories you are authorized to access. The tool runs with the current operating-system permissions. Review findings and suggested corrections before changing files; the rule is narrow and may miss other drift or produce an incorrect match. Sign-in does not grant access to another person's computer, account, or repository.

Do not rely on DocuGuard as the sole security, legal, compliance, or release review for production or high-impact systems. Do not use the preview to process data you are not permitted to inspect.

## Availability and changes

This is an early prototype supplied as-is for evaluation. There is no uptime commitment, paid plan, or support service. No public hosted service is active until separately deployed and reviewed.

These plain-language terms describe the preview. They are not a reviewed commercial agreement or legal advice.
